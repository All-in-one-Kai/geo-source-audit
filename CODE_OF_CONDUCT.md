# Community conduct

Discuss reproducible behaviour and ideas respectfully, not people's identities.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, credential exposure and unsolicited sales spam
are not welcome. Respect contributors' boundaries and do not publish private information.
Maintainers may edit or remove unsafe content and restrict disruptive participation.
Report a concern privately to kai@ki-agenten.shop with a sanitized description.
This original policy applies to this repository, not a claim of external certification.
